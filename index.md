# Core Hermit – On-Device AI

Chat with and compare **Apple Intelligence** and **open AI models** that run entirely on your iPhone or iPad. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and your conversations never leave your device.

- **Private by design:** everything runs on the device. The network is used only to download the model files you choose.
- **Compare models:** measure speed on your own device and judge answer quality in blind side-by-side comparisons.
- **Works offline:** once a model is downloaded, it runs without a connection.
