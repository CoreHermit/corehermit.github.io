# Core Hermit Support

**Contact:** [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}?subject=Core%20Hermit%20Support). We read every message. Please include your device model and iOS version.

To report a problematic AI response, long-press it in the app and choose **Report Response**. This opens a pre-filled email; nothing is sent unless you send it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which devices are supported?**
Core Hermit runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 26 or later.
- **Apple Intelligence, Compare and Quality** need a device that supports Apple Intelligence (for example iPhone 15 Pro and later, or an iPad with an M-series chip), with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri.
- **On other devices,** Core Hermit works as a private, offline chat app with open models.

**How do I get an open model?**
Tap the model name at the top of a chat and choose a model, then tap **Download**. Models are 0.6–3 GB, so Wi-Fi is recommended. Downloads continue in the background. Each model needs a minimum amount of device memory (3–8 GB); models your device can't run are shown as unavailable.

**Does anything I type leave my device?**
No. Chats, documents and voice input are processed on your device. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy/).

**Apple Intelligence says it's unavailable.**
Check that your device supports it, that it's turned on, and that its model has finished downloading (this can take a while after you first turn it on). The app shows the exact reason in Settings › This Device.

**Voice mode replies before I've finished.**
Open Voice Settings › Listening › **Pause before replying** and choose **Long**. A ring around the button shows when it's about to send; keep talking to continue.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete individual chats, documents, models, quality votes and performance history in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
