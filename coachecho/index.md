# CoachEcho – Lesson Recorder

Private coaching memory for tennis and cricket coaches, from [Core Hermit]({{ site.baseurl }}/).

Record a lesson, even with the screen locked. Get a transcript made on your iPhone, and a draft coaching report you review before you share it. Your recordings, players and reports stay on your device. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.

- **Record anywhere:** long sessions in a pocket, with start and stop tones, or from your Apple Watch.
- **Transcribed on your iPhone:** timestamped and linked to the audio.
- **Draft reports you review:** written with Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones. Counts and trends are calculated by the app.
- **People and progress:** see recurring feedback for each player over time.
