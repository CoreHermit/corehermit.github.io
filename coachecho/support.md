# CoachEcho Support

Email **{{ site.support_email }}** with questions, bug reports, or a problem with a transcript or report. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. Don't send recordings of other people unless they've agreed.

## FAQ

**Which devices does CoachEcho need?**
An iPhone with iOS 17 or later records, transcribes, and exports. Draft reports and session chat need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later), with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 10 or later.

**Why is "Generate Report" unavailable?**
Apple Intelligence is off, still downloading, or not supported on this iPhone. CoachEcho shows the reason. Recording and transcripts still work.

**Does recording continue when the screen locks?**
Yes. CoachEcho keeps recording in the background, and its tones (and optional voice announcements in earphones) confirm start, pause, and stop.

**Where is my data?**
Only on your iPhone. You can see the recordings folder in the Files app (On My iPhone › CoachEcho). Use Sessions › Export or a backup to keep a copy before deleting the app. Deleting the app deletes its data.

**Is my audio sent anywhere?**
No, as long as On-Device Recognition is on (the default). If you turn it off, iOS may send audio to Apple to transcribe it. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/coachecho/privacy/).

**Do I need permission to record players?**
Yes. Follow the recording laws where you coach; some places require everyone's consent. For children, ask a parent or guardian.

**Are the reports accurate?**
They're AI drafts and can contain mistakes. Check them against the recording before sharing. See the [AI Disclaimer]({{ site.baseurl }}/coachecho/ai-disclaimer/).
